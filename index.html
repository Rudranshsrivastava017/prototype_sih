WHY CONVECTX? OVERCOMING SHORT-TERM CONVECTIVE BLIND SPOTS

Understanding why traditional numerical weather prediction struggles with localized 0–6 hour severe convective storms.

PROBLEM STATEMENT SIH26084

TRADITIONAL WEATHER INFORMATION

  • Broad-scale numerical forecast: NWP models run on 6-to-12-hour assimilation cycles, missing rapid sub-hourly storm evolutions.
  • Limited local spatial detail: 3 km–12 km grids smooth out intense cloudburst cores and violent 500-meter microbursts.
  • Spin-up delay: Physical atmospheric equations require 1–3 hours of simulation spin-up, creating a critical nowcasting blind spot.
  • Short-lived convective evolution: Convective cells form, drop lethal lightning, and dissipate in 30–60 minutes—well before standard models refresh.

CONVECTX AI NOWCASTING APPROACH

  • Multi-source real-time observation: Ingests Doppler radar, INSAT cloud-top cooling, and lightning surges simultaneously.
  • Real-time feature fusion: Aligns disparate physical feeds into a unified 500-meter geo-temporal tensor grid every 5 minutes.
  • Early Convective Initiation (CI): Detects explosive updrafts before heavy rain reaches the surface, adding 30–45 min of lead time.
  • 0–6 Hour Spatio-Temporal Nowcasting: Deep neural models extrapolate storm trajectories, cell decay, and new initiation.
  • Hyperlocal Risk & Early Warnings: Generates actionable, coordinate-specific alerts for district emergency teams and citizens.
